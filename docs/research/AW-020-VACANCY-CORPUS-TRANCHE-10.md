`document/event arrives → validate fields/state → transfer/update in 1C/ERP/database → create standard result/document → monitor status → reconcile → exception`

But the gate **still does not prove**:

- buyer willingness to outsource those subflows;
- willingness to pay Arvectum Work;
- machine-executable share of a whole role;
- customer verification burden;
- Work acceptance rate;
- contribution margin;
- repeat purchase.

Those remain later evidence gates.
