## Current interpretation

The strongest repeated pattern is not "AI specialist" demand. It is employers paying people to move structured information between documents and business systems while detecting exceptions.

This reinforces the current leading Work pattern:

`high-volume inputs → deterministic normalization/reconciliation → accepted clean-path output → small exception queue`

The next corpus tranche should prioritize more exact vacancy-level records from additional sources and gather volume clues, because salary without workload volume is not sufficient for pricing.
