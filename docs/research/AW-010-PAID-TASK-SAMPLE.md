## 1. Purpose

It is **not** buyer interview evidence, does not prove willingness to pay for Arvectum Work, and does not establish that an autonomous Executor can deliver the same work economically.

## 3. Platform acceptance context

FL.ru listings frequently expose a `Гарантия возврата` marker and define a fixed price, time and result package. Kwork publicly states that the seller is paid only after the buyer checks and accepts the order, and its offer pages expose refund/revision mechanics.

This demonstrates that **result acceptance as a commercial interaction is already familiar** on Russian freelance platforms. It does not prove that acceptance can be made objective enough for autonomous machine work.

- https://kwork.ru/terms_of_service
- https://www.fl.ru/uslugi-freelancera/

## 4. Observed patterns

### Fact: bounded output units exist across several candidate Job families

The sample is not limited to software development. Public fixed-price offers exist for:
- web data extraction;
- data cleanup and normalization;
- document conversion / structured processing;
- bounded code fixes;
- recurring monitoring;
- research/report output;
- procurement-adjacent specification reconciliation.

### Fact: many packages already expose acceptance-friendly boundaries

Common observable boundaries include:
- one source/site/file/project;
- maximum records/rows/pages/fields;
- one small bug or one bounded code change;
- named output format;
- one revision round;
- explicit treatment of ambiguous records instead of guessing.

These are useful inputs to future Arvectum Work Job and Acceptance design.

### Fact: public prices cover a wide range

This sample spans 500 RUB to 19,500 RUB for relatively bounded operational tasks, with higher-ticket research/procurement analysis visible at 65,000 RUB. Price alone does not show transaction volume or realized willingness to pay.

### Hypothesis: J-02/J-03/J-06 may be easier to verify than open-ended research

Document/data transformation and monitoring expose more machine-checkable fields, limits and artifacts than open-ended market research. This is only a verification hypothesis; buyer demand evidence is still missing.

### Hypothesis: some current human-service offers are plausible Mac mini Worker benchmarks

Examples such as table cleanup, PDF→structured file processing, public-site extraction and deterministic monitoring appear technically suitable for a bounded local Worker experiment. This does not establish positive unit economics or buyer acceptance.

## 5. Biases and limitations

- This first tranche is deliberately convenience-sampled and heavily represented by FL.ru because its public pages expose current price/scope/time clearly.
- Offer listing is **supply evidence**, not proof that the offer has sold at the listed price.
- Visible counters/reviews were not used as revenue proof.
- Public platform refund/acceptance mechanics do not prove task-level objective acceptance.
- No personal contact, private buyer data or fabricated transaction evidence is included.

## 6. AW-010-B conclusion

Task-level acceptance criteria are satisfied:
- 15 concrete public examples;
- multiple candidate Job families;
- price/package/acceptance signals recorded where visible;
- every example has a source and common observation date (`2026-09-17`);
- unknown or indirect acceptance is not upgraded to evidence;
- no example is treated as willingness-to-pay proof for Arvectum Work.

This does **not** complete AW-010. The next research task remains `AW-010-C`: extend the evidence set to 30+ examples and synthesize patterns/counterexamples before any stage judgment.
