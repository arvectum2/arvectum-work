## 4. Strategic synthesis

The Rabota.ru tranche reinforces three recurring machine-work shapes:

1. **document/order operations**  
   `input → validate → reflect in 1C/ERP → create/track docs → reconcile → exception`

2. **structured data maintenance**  
   `source file/document → field extraction → normalization → database/card update → validation → report`

3. **catalog/content operations**  
   `source product data → card/attribute/content preparation → upload/update → QA → exception`

The key boundary is consistent across sources: the automatable slice is repetitive and acceptance-friendly, while communication, ambiguous judgment, access authority and business accountability remain with a Principal/human function.
