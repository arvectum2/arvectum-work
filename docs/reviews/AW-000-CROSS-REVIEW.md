## 3. Specific challenge review

### Challenge A — Are we prematurely assuming a two-sided marketplace?

No. `H-08` explicitly treats marketplace value as a later hypothesis. The roadmap allows the viable business to turn out to be a managed machine-work service instead.

### Challenge B — Are we treating local/self-hosted execution as proven buyer value?

No. It remains a Russia-first differentiation hypothesis and must be evidenced in buyer discovery.

### Challenge C — Is digital ruble availability being overstated?

No. Official 2026 rollout facts are recorded, but exact bank/customer/transaction availability is a re-verification gate. AW-050 does not require digital rubles.

### Challenge D — Are 44-FZ/223-FZ paths assumed?

No. The baseline explicitly rejects generic applicability claims and requires transaction-specific legal/procurement review.

### Challenge E — Could the Mac mini experiment pass by hiding Owner labor?

No. Human intervention categories and minutes are mandatory evidence; material human completion cannot be labeled autonomous.

### Challenge F — Is first paid transaction too late?

No. AW-040 is designed so payment may occur immediately. AW-050 exists to ensure funds actually settle and whole-transaction economics are measured before viability review.

### Challenge G — Are stage counts becoming vanity metrics?

No. Interview/task counts are stated as stopping heuristics; evidence strength ladder gives paid/repeat behavior precedence.
