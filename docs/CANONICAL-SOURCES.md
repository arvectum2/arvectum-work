## 1. Authority and source order

Within their applicable scopes:

1. applicable law and valid corporate/legal authority;
2. approved Arvectum Company decisions for Company/portfolio/capital/risk authority;
3. approved Arvectum Work product decisions and Product Contracts within product scope;
4. applicable canonical Arvectum OS Constitution/RFC/ADR/Product Contracts where Work actually consumes OS;
5. verified Work implementation and product/experiment evidence;
6. Work roadmap as a planning source, not independent authority;
7. research artifacts as evidence subject to source quality/freshness;
8. current task/chat/model memory/local/generated materials as context until explicitly promoted.

Technical access, credentials or repository permissions do not create Organizational Authority.

## 6. Evidence discipline

The repository must distinguish:

- `Fact` — supported by current source/evidence;
- `Hypothesis` — testable but unproven;
- `Decision` — attributable authority action;
- `Evidence` — observation from an experiment/transaction;
- `Unknown` — material value not yet established.

Unknown MUST NOT silently become zero, false or assumed-safe.

## 7. Public repository boundary

Do not commit:

- secrets/private keys/reusable credentials;
- bank credentials or raw transaction payloads;
- unnecessary personal data;
- customer confidential documents;
- non-public exact commercial terms where disclosure is not authorized;
- privileged legal/security incident details;
- chain-of-thought.

Use privacy-safe evidence references for paid-transaction proof.
