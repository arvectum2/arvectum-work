# Product Decision 002 — Vacancy-first Discovery before outbound interviews

Status: `Accepted for current Discovery sequencing`
Date: `2026-09-17`
Scope: `Arvectum Work`

## Decision

Before spending effort on broad buyer interviews, Arvectum Work will use the current Russian vacancy market as a public demand proxy to identify recurring business functions that already have observable labor budgets.

The immediate Discovery question becomes:

> Which recurring digital functions are employers hiring humans to perform today, and for which of those functions can Arvectum Work plausibly offer a cheaper accepted-work stream while preserving bounded human exception handling?

## Why

A vacancy is stronger than generic AI-interest evidence because it shows an employer is actively allocating or attempting to allocate budget to a function.

Vacancy evidence still does not prove willingness to outsource or buy Arvectum Work.

## Required method

Analyze vacancies by atomic duties, not job titles.

For each function estimate:

- observable salary/range;
- recurrence/volume clues;
- structured inputs and outputs;
- current systems such as 1C/ЭДО/CRM/Excel;
- automatable recurring slice;
- human-only judgment/communication/accountability slice;
- acceptance/evidence path;
- likely exception classes;
- candidate Work pricing basis;
- employee + modern AI substitute;
- owned-automation substitute and crossover.

## Sequencing consequence

`AW-020-C-REAL-INTERVIEWS` is deferred, not cancelled.

Before it becomes primary again, Work should complete a vacancy/function corpus and select the top work-stream hypotheses for focused validation.

Interviews, when resumed, should target employers/functions identified by evidence rather than a broad generic buyer sample.

## Business boundary

Do not claim `100k salary → 50k Work` as an established saving.

A role must be decomposed into:

`automatable flow + exceptions + judgment/communication + accountability`.

The real comparison is:

`current attributable flow cost`

versus

`Work price + residual customer handling`

versus

`owned automation amortization/support + residual human handling`.

## Cross-product boundary

If evidence shows a stable high-volume process is economically better owned by the customer than purchased through Work, Work should identify a handoff signal to Arvectum Company. Arvectum Work does not itself own the separate owned-agent / Arvectum OS product.
