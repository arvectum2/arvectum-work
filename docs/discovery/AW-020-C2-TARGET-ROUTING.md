## Route-use rule

Even for READY ROUTE targets:

- do not imply the public inbox/form is the process owner;
- ask for routing to the owner of the relevant operation;
- do not mention a vacancy applicant context;
- do not submit through hh/Rabota job-response mechanisms;
- do not attach files or request confidential data in the first message;
- one initial message + at most one approved follow-up only;
- suppress immediately on opt-out or indication that the route is inappropriate.
