# AW-000 — Company / Arvectum Work / Arvectum OS Boundary Reconciliation

Status: `Proposed 0.1.0`
Date: `2026-09-17`
Canonical product repository: `arvectum2/arvectum-work`
Parent Company repository: `arvectum2/arvectum-company`
Platform repository: `arvectum2/arvectum-os`

## 1. Reconciliation result

The former working hypothesis `Arvectum Exchange / AX` was drafted in Arvectum Company as an unmerged discovery proposal.

The Owner has now directed creation of **Arvectum Work** as a separate product project.

Therefore:

- the former AX draft is historical discovery context, not the canonical product home;
- product-specific discovery and semantics are owned by `arvectum2/arvectum-work`;
- Company retains only Company-level portfolio/investment/governance authority;
- no Arvectum OS Product Contract or capability is created by this reconciliation.

## 2. What remains in Arvectum Company

Company SHOULD keep only a bounded footprint sufficient to answer:

- does Arvectum Work exist as a portfolio initiative/product discovery;
- what canonical repository owns it;
- what Company Position is accountable for portfolio/product oversight;
- what Company capital/risk/priority envelope applies;
- what decisions remain Reserved Owner Decisions;
- when Company must decide `Continue / Pivot / Stop`.

Company MUST NOT become the canonical store for:

- Job taxonomy;
- buyer interview evidence;
- marketplace semantics;
- Assignment/Execution/Result/Acceptance domain model;
- product unit economics;
- Worker experiment details;
- product architecture/code.

## 3. What belongs in Arvectum Work

Arvectum Work owns:

- Product Vision and hypotheses;
- discovery plans and evidence;
- customer/problem research;
- competitor/market research;
- job taxonomy and acceptance design;
- business model and unit economics;
- product-specific Principal/Executor/Job/Assignment/Execution/Result/Evidence/Acceptance/Settlement semantics;
- Worker experiments;
- future Product Contract proposals;
- product architecture, implementation and operational evidence.

Work may not create Company authority merely by documenting a need.

## 4. What belongs in Arvectum OS

Arvectum OS remains canonical for shared domain-neutral platform contracts/capabilities admitted through OS governance.

Possible future reusable capability areas may include:

- identity;
- authority/authorization representation;
- records/relationships;
- provenance/evidence primitives;
- governed execution;
- documents/knowledge;
- security/isolation;
- persistence/observability.

No such dependency is inferred merely because Work has conceptually similar entities.

The following remain **product semantics** unless a later OS governance process proves a domain-neutral reusable contract:

- Job marketplace;
- matching/bidding;
- machine-work pricing;
- capability-market ranking;
- paid Assignment semantics;
- Acceptance rules for marketplace Jobs;
- worker payout/take-rate;
- disputes specific to paid machine work;
- marketplace settlement orchestration.

## 5. Promotion rule from Work to OS

A Work-local pattern may be proposed to OS only when:

1. it has demonstrated value in real product evidence or is independently required for platform governance/security/interoperability;
2. the proposed contract can be expressed without machine-work marketplace semantics;
3. an OS RFC/Product Contract/other applicable governance path explicitly accepts it;
4. ownership, lifecycle, compatibility and migration consequences are clear.

Until then, bounded reversible product-local implementation is preferred.

## 6. Former Arvectum Exchange draft disposition

Company PR `arvectum2/arvectum-company#3` (`AX-000: start Arvectum Exchange hypothesis discovery`) is superseded by this separate-product direction.

Reusable ideas carried forward into Work include:

- verifiable task outcome as the traded unit;
- Principal remains legal/economic counterparty;
- Russia-first RUB/SBP/digital-ruble/data-sovereignty hypotheses;
- first Worker may use the existing 24/7 local-LLM host;
- evidence before marketplace build;
- no unverified procurement/compliance claims.

Changed from the AX draft:

- canonical product name is `Arvectum Work`, prefix `AW`;
- product discovery is in a dedicated product repository now, not Company;
- first real paid transaction is a distinct gate before marketplace viability;
- marketplace software is explicitly not admitted until AW-060 GO;
- Company promotion is no longer deferred until the end of AX discovery: Company may recognize Work as a bounded discovery portfolio initiative while preserving investment gates.

## 7. Authority boundary

The following require the applicable Company/legal/OS authority and are not granted by Work roadmap status:

- material capital/spend;
- contracts and material commercial commitments beyond existing authority;
- public launch;
- custody/escrow/payment-provider commitments;
- production deployment with material external effect;
- legal/risk exceptions;
- public-procurement participation/submission;
- Company↔Product↔OS boundary changes;
- OS RFC/Product Contract acceptance.

## 8. Discovery operating rule

The cheapest reversible experiment that produces stronger commercial evidence is preferred over platform construction.

The current value target is:

`real paid accepted result + measured economics + credible repeat demand`.
